How to play
  • Draw one continuous line that fills every cell of the grid.
  • The line must pass through the numbered cells in order: 1, then 2, then 3…
  • Move only up, down, left or right — never diagonally, never over yourself.
  • Drag from the 1 to draw. Drag back along the line, or tap a cell you already used, to rub it out.

How to play
  • Fill every cell with a sun or a moon — click a cell to cycle.
  • Each row and column holds an equal number of each.
  • No three in a row of the same symbol, horizontally or vertically.
  • = between two cells means they match; × means they differ.
  • Every puzzle is solvable by pure logic — you never have to guess.
